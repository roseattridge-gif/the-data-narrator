The Canvas

Use one page to carry an audience from evidence they can see to a decision they are prepared to make. Complete it before building the deck.

01 · AUDIENCE

Who must cross?

Who needs to understand, believe or act? What do they already know, value, fear or resist?

02 · DECISION

What must happen?

Name the specific choice, commitment or behaviour required. Avoid vague aims such as “raise awareness”.

03 · SIGNAL

What is the truth?

What is the smallest set of evidence that proves the important point? What can be removed without weakening it?

04 · STAKES

Why does it matter?

Translate the evidence through time, cost, quality, risk and human consequence. What changes if nothing changes?

05 · BRIDGE

How will meaning travel?

Choose the contrast, sequence, analogy or visual that makes the truth understandable and repeatable.

06 · FRICTION

What makes this difficult?

What objection, trade-off, missing context or uncomfortable implication must be acknowledged rather than hidden?

07 · THE ASK

Finish the sentence.

“Having seen this evidence, I need this audience to…”
