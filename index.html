Why this book

Dashboards do not make decisions. People do.

I have watched brilliant analysts explain the same finding three different ways and still leave the room without a decision. I have watched leaders drown in reporting while the most important signal sat in plain sight. The problem was not intelligence. It was the gap between evidence and action.

The Data Narrator closes that gap. It helps you find what matters, understand the human stakes and build a story sturdy enough to carry other people towards a choice. Because being right is not the same as being heard—and being heard is not the same as changing what happens next.

The connection

Put It on the Wall makes a complicated system visible to the people inside it. The Data Narrator makes the meaning visible to the people who must decide. Different offers; the same lifelong instinct: get the truth out of one person’s head and into a form everyone can work with.

The method

The Bridge carries people from a number they can see to a decision they will make.

A data story is not decoration applied to analysis. It is a crossing: from where your audience is now to somewhere useful they could not reach from the numbers alone.

EvidenceMeaningAction
01

The near bank

Start where your audience really is. Find the signal, the decision in front of them and the outcome they already care about.

02

The span

Select the evidence, contrast and analogy that make the meaning travel—without making people wade through every piece of working.

03

The far bank

Land on a specific choice. Name who must act, what changes next and what accepting the truth may cost. The far bank is human, not merely logical.

04

The Canvas

Put the whole crossing on one page: audience, stakes, signal, story and ask. Use it for an insight, meeting, metric, paper or board decision.

The toolkit

Practical tools drawn from rooms where the answer had to land.
